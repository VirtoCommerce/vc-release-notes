What's inside every deck

Built for decision-making, not slideshows

Every deck follows the same interactive pattern — designed so product, engineering, and business stakeholders can review what shipped and turn it into a concrete backlog in minutes.

Business-first ordering

"WOW Business Features" always come first with a golden divider. Executives and PMs get impact up front, not buried under platform notes.

T-shirt sizing

S · M · L per feature. Estimates integration complexity, not calendar time — your team's context decides the days.

Backlog export

Tap + Add to backlog on any feature, hit Copy as Markdown, paste straight into Jira, Linear, or GitHub Issues.

Table of contents

Every deck has a slide-out TOC panel — jump between sections, preview features, or navigate by feature title. Press T to open.

Keyboard + touch

← → navigate · B add to backlog · F fullscreen · T contents. Swipe left/right on mobile. Tap any image to zoom.

Zero dependencies

Pure HTML/CSS/JS in a single file. Clone, open, or drop on Netlify. No build, no framework, no CDN scripts required.
